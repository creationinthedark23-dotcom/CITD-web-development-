BlackSalt

Kitchenware & culinary products Operating

Chef apparel, culinary gear and kitchen products for professionals who care as much about how equipment works as how it looks.

The brand

BlackSalt develops chef apparel, culinary gear and hospitality-focused products for people who work in kitchens rather than photograph them. The starting point is always the working condition — heat, movement, laundry cycles, long shifts — and the design follows from there.

It is built for professional kitchens, culinary classrooms and the wider culture around them: the people learning the craft, the people teaching it, and the people who have been doing it long enough to be particular about what they wear and what they use.

What the group holds

Creation in the Dark Holdings owns BlackSalt, holds its brand and product intellectual property, and funds its development. Product decisions, supply relationships and the day-to-day of the business sit with the brand.
