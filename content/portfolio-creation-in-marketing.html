Creation in Marketing

Brand & creative services Operating

Brand identity, creative systems and digital experiences, for businesses that need a brand capable of carrying commercial weight.

The brand

Creation in Marketing builds brand identities, creative systems and digital experiences — the work that lets a business be recognised, understood and chosen. It runs from strategy through to execution rather than handing over a deck and leaving.

The through-line is structure. An identity that cannot be applied consistently is a drawing, not a brand. The work is finished when a client can keep using it without the people who made it.

What the group holds

Creation in the Dark Holdings owns Creation in Marketing and holds its brand. Client work, creative direction and delivery sit with the brand.

It is also the group's own studio: the identity systems used across the portfolio are developed here, which is why brands that leave this building tend to arrive fully formed.
