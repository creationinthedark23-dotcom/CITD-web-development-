BlackSalt KitchenWEAR

Chefwear and culinary gear made for people who actually work in kitchens.

The brand

BlackSalt KitchenWEAR makes chefwear, culinary gear and kitchen products for people who work in kitchens rather than photograph them. The starting point is always the working condition — heat, movement, laundry cycles, twelve-hour shifts — and the design follows from there.

It is built for professional kitchens, culinary classrooms and the culture around them: the people learning the craft, the people teaching it, and the people who have been at it long enough to be particular about what they wear.
